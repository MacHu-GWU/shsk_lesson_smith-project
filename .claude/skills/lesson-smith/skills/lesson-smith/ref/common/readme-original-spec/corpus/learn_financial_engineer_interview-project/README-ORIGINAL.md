# learn_financial_engineer_interview-project

Financial engineering, or quant work as everyone actually calls it, sits where finance, mathematics, and technology meet. Banks, hedge funds, and asset managers bet real money on pricing models, risk systems, and trading strategies, and that's what these interviews are really testing. The bar is high. Nobody asks you for a definition. They drop you into a scenario and watch how you think, and that kind of judgment doesn't come from reading a few books here and there.

This repo is a bank of 264 interview questions, each with a full write-up, sorted into Entry, Mid-Level, and Senior so you can work at the depth your target role calls for. Get through it and an unfamiliar model stops being a wall: you can say why it holds, name the assumption that breaks first, and reach for the right tool once it does. That's the advanced tier, enough to carry a serious quant interview and enough to hold your own on your first real project once you're in the seat.

Two ways to use it. With Claude Code installed, type `/practice-cn`: the agent picks the questions, runs you through them in interview mode or study mode, gives feedback on every answer, and closes with a score and your weak spots. Without the agent, the bank is plain Markdown. Pick a question from `questions-index.md` and read.
